In an [Umebayashi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xv-uSjjENvw) induced [comagasm](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=McClAvitHk4), the ATM queue snakes along the half unpainted chain-link fence, off absentminded Cuticura tracks and lucid dreams of a two-thousand-Rupee paperback, freshly minted. The girl in faded jeans and white dress-shirt and unintended RayBans finds a glass of black tea (or was it coffee) in the third canteen she terrorises. Everybody is happy, sings the last two stanzas of whatever they remember off _Virinjuninna Parilum_ and goes their parallel ways. Sometimes they meet for clandestine shopping complex combings and sing _Partisookthangal_ to each other over _Parippuvadas_ and black tea they find in the third canteen the girl terrorises.
