See [La Belle Verte](https://vimeo.com/214163453) (The Green Beautiful) and [Down from the Mountains](https://vimeo.com/245003961), from 1996 and 2017.

The Green Beautiful explores the idea of an utopia holding a fine mirror to our broken reality. The film is silly and self-aware. Down from the Mountains is the tale of a family separated by mountains and money. A mother in _Verte_ quips ‘but they don’t have lipstick’ in a moment that refuses to linger with delusions of grandeur. In _Mountains_, the mother of six holds banknotes against the light as she double-checks the sum she is paid for peppercorns and wonders if it wouldn’t be great if she didn’t have to.
